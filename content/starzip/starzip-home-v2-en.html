FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions
Quick answers to common questions
What is Starzip?+
Starzip is a decentralized exchange (DEX) where local stores — cafés, restaurants, gyms — issue their own tokens, and customers can buy and trade them. Stores raise funds from their community without banks; customers participate in the growth of stores they love.
What is the Launchpad?+
The Launchpad is a public token offering before a store lists on the DEX. Participate with USDT/USDC to receive tokens at the offering price. Stake STAR for up to a 2.0× allocation boost. If the soft cap is not met, a full automatic refund is issued.
Is the gas fee really $0?+
On-chain actions in the business owner console (token issuance, disclosures, voting) have their gas covered by Paymaster (up to $1/month per owner). The opBNB network itself charges only ~$0.001 per transaction — among the lowest in the industry.
How do I list my store?+
You can apply directly or be referred by a partner. Any brick-and-mortar store operating for at least 6 months with a valid business registration can apply. Review takes 2–4 weeks.
Are my assets safe?+
All assets are held in smart contracts on the opBNB blockchain. Starzip is non-custodial — we never hold your funds. An independent security audit will be completed before mainnet deployment.
